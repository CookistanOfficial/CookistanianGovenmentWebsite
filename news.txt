[HEADLINE NEWS]
NATIONAL CELEBRATIONS HELD FOR INDEPENDENCE DAY
PORT COOKIE — Citizens across the Republic gathered to celebrate 
Cookistan's Official Independence. Festivities included community 
gatherings, parades, and broadcast renditions of the national anthem, 
"Boogie Down" by Grey Room. 

[LOCAL UPDATES]
OFFICIAL ANIMAL RECOGNITION CEREMONY
The Ministry of Culture hosted a ceremony honoring the Munchkin House 
Cat as the official National Animal of Cookistan. A permanent park 
sanctuary in the capital district has been dedicated in recognition of 
the national mascot.

[GOVERNMENT NOTICE]
DIGITAL CITIZENSHIP PORTAL OPEN TO NEW APPLICANTS
The Department of Foreign & Internal Affairs reminds prospective 
citizens that applications for Digital Citizenship are actively being 
reviewed. Interested individuals are encouraged to submit their form 
via the official state web portal.

[DIPLOMACY]
COOKISTAN SEEKS NEW INTERNATIONAL ALLIANCES
State representatives are currently accepting diplomatic inquiries from 
partner micro-nations and allied digital communities. Inquiries may be 
submitted through official government channels.
