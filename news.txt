10/15/26
Cookistan has recently made several important developments in its efforts to establish and organize the Federal Republic of Cookistan.

One major development was the revocation of the citizenship of Cookistan’s first digital citizen. Following a decision to revoke the citizenship, the individual’s status as a Cookistani digital citizen was withdrawn. This marks an important event in Cookistan’s citizenship history and demonstrates that digital citizenship is subject to the country’s rules and decisions.

Cookistan is also making plans for a government building at its headquarters. The planned structure is intended to provide a dedicated place for government activities and help establish a more organized setting for the nation’s administration. Current ideas include a small, low-cost structure using tables, seating, a flag, and suitable materials for a roof and walls. The project is still in the planning stage, with affordability and practicality being important considerations.

In addition to these developments, Cookistan continues to work on its national identity, including its flags, currency system, national symbols, and online presence. These efforts are part of Cookistan’s ongoing attempt to build its institutions and document its progress as a micronation.

Further updates will be published as new developments take place.
