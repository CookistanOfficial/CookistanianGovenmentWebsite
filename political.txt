GOVERNMENT GAZETTE OF THE REPUBLIC OF COOKISTAN
OFFICIAL STATUTES, DECREES & CONSTITUTIONAL ACTS
===================================================================

[STATUTE 2026-01: DECLARATION OF SOVEREIGNTY]
Be it known to all citizens and foreign observers that the Republic 
of Cookistan is established as a free, sovereign digital micronation, 
governed by principles of community engagement, creativity, and peaceful 
coexistence.

[ACT 2026-02: NATIONAL SYMBOLS ACT]
Section 1. The Official Flag of Cookistan shall feature the national 
colors with a central chocolate-chip cookie emblem.
Section 2. The Official National Anthem is hereby declared to be 
"Boogie Down" by Grey Room, designed to promote rhythm and groove across 
all territories.
Section 3. The Munchkin House Cat is designated as the sole National 
Animal of the Republic.

[DECREE 2026-03: DIGITAL CITIZENSHIP FRAMEWORK]
Citizenship in Cookistan is open to digital residents worldwide who 
complete the standard verification process. All digital citizens maintain 
the right to participate in state polls, view state archives, and receive 
official updates from the Government Gazette.
